Semantic embeddings

Every article becomes a meaning-based vector after you fetch (automatic background job). Search, screening, and clusters all use those fingerprints.

Screening & triage

Narrow it down after a fetch, group papers by theme, or mark individual records Not relevant. Search ranks the papers you keep.
