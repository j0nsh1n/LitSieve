How it works

Simple

One Search page

Fetch by topic, wait while papers prepare, write your real question in Narrow it down, then rank and export — no stepper.

Advanced

Every control

Get papers → Clusters → Clean up → Search, plus source picks, seed paper, and ranking options.

Guides

Eight short explainers — click a card to open the full page.

01

Multi-source search

Pull articles from 17 databases at once — PubMed, Europe PMC, arXiv, OpenAlex, Semantic Scholar, and more. Simple collects on Search; Advanced uses Get papers.

Learn more → 02

Semantic embeddings

Every article becomes a meaning-based vector after you fetch (automatic background job). Search, screening, and clusters all use those fingerprints.

Learn more → 03

Screening & triage

Simple: Narrow it down opens after fetch (required before Search). Advanced: group by topic on Clusters, or Quick screen on Clean up. Search only ranks what you kept.

Learn more → 04

Clean up

Remove near-duplicates across sources, optional Quick screen, and a screening report for hand-ins. Simple shows collected / dups / screened-out / kept on Search.

Learn more → 05

Similarity search

Rank by meaning plus exact words, mark Not relevant, export RIS for Zotero. Advanced adds seed paper and ranking options.

Learn more → 06

Private workspace

Private accounts, named libraries, Simple/Advanced layout toggle, a guest demo, background jobs, and private notes and stars.

Learn more → 07

Finish your research

Public databases are a starting point. Continue with your school library, Google Scholar, and a librarian — without paywalled campus packages we do not have.

Learn more → 08

Citation quality

Manual checklist: peer-reviewed vs preprint vs website, plus weak appraisal signals only — never clinical evidence grades.
