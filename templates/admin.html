About this page

The snapshot is counts only (no full roster). Search by username, email, or account id. Queues are short lists: locked, unverified, over quota, recent failed sign-ins.

Password reset and one-time sign-in links are emailed to a verified address. This console never shows or sets a password, and it does not sign in as them.

Destructive actions ask you to type the username and a reason. Every action is logged on that account’s timeline.
