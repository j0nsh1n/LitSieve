About this page

The snapshot is counts only (no full roster). Search by username, email, or account id. Queues are short lists: locked, unverified, over quota, recent failed sign-ins, and stuck fetch/prepare jobs.

Password reset and one-time sign-in links are emailed to a verified address. Changing an email sends a new verification link; this console never marks the address verified, and it never shows codes or tokens.

Every action asks for a reason and lands on that account’s timeline. Actions that change access or delete data also ask you to type the username; publishing or taking down the banner asks you to type publish. Job retry runs on the student’s library, not yours.
