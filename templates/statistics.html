About this page

Remove duplicates finds near-identical papers across sources so you keep one copy. Auto-resolve prefers the most complete abstract (and stronger sources when lengths are similar). Redundant copies are screened out (hidden from Search), not deleted.

Quick screen ranks against your research question and can screen out the least-related set; preview first if you want. Prefer topic groups? Advanced users can still open Clusters.

The screening report is plain-text counts for hand-ins (PRISMA-style), not a bibliography — use Search → RIS for Zotero citations.
