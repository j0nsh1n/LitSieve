About this page

Pick topics (and sources in Advanced), type a research question, then Fetch Articles. Preparing for search runs automatically afterward. You can leave the page open while progress runs.

Sources are free public databases, not a college library package. Some may fail (rate limits) while others succeed — that is normal. Coverage map shows empty recommended sources after a fetch.

Preparing creates embeddings (meaning fingerprints) for Search, Clusters, and duplicates. Re-prepare is optional if that step failed or you change the model. Guest demos load sample papers only (no multi-source fetch).

After prepare finishes, you may get a short screening step (Low / Medium / High), then Go to Search. Guest demos use sample papers only.
